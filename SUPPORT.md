# Support

## Bugs and feature requests

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/PredictabilityAtScale/usagetap-model-lifecycle-action/issues) with:

- The action version or full commit SHA.
- The relevant workflow configuration with secrets removed.
- Expected and actual behavior.
- A minimal model-key or file-format example when possible.

General UsageTap product and account support is available through the [UsageTap support page](https://usagetap.com/support).

## Security

Do not report vulnerabilities in a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Support expectations

This open-source action is provided under the MIT License without a service-level commitment. Paid or enterprise support, if available, is governed by the applicable UsageTap agreement.
